About

CaterinaAlvarez

Editor and writer

In 2016, I got an idea to write a book and started studying the art and craft of fiction like crazy at a not-for-profit devoted to the literary arts. I got a few stories published and began reading the slush pile for several literary journals.

One of them, an indie press, hired me as an editor in 2022. Another, an online magazine that publishes one work of fiction a day 365 days a year, also hired me per my application.

Soon, authors whom I served for these publications asked if I could help them on the side with their works-in-progress. I discovered I adore this type of work: I love stories and people and writing and working on my own time in spaces of my choice, often from far-flung places.

Biography

Ms. Alvarez’s work appears in The Los Angeles Review, The Atticus Review, Flash Boulevard, Tiny Molecules, and other journals. Her story “Short Division” won a spot on the Wigleaf Top 50 for 2022. She is a senior editor at Thirty West Publishing House and a priority editor for Flash Fiction Magazine. A longtime member of the Lighthouse Writers Workshop, she received her MA from USC Annenberg School in 2000.

Grand Avenue

With Grand Avenue Editing & Publications, she seeks to reach a broader audience for her publications and to provide the deep and thorough feedback she provides to clients as an editor at both Flash Fiction Magazine and Thirty West Publishing House.
