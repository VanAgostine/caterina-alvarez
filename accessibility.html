Accessibility

Last reviewed [date]

Our aim

Grand Avenue Editing & Publications wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What the website does

  • Pages use a clear structure of headings, landmarks and lists.
  • Meaningful images have text descriptions.
  • Every link, button and form control can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible outline showing where you are.
  • A “skip to content” link is available at the top of each page.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the website’s animations become gentler.

If something is difficult to use

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or would like information in another format, please email caterina@grandavenueediting.com and describe the problem and the page it is on.
