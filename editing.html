The approach

I drop into stories hunting around for meaning and obscurity. I am intuitive about what works and what doesn’t; what is okay to hide in a story and what must be exposed for the reader to understand what lies at its heart.

I am a senior editor at Thirty West Publishing House and a priority editor for Flash Fiction Magazine.

With Grand Avenue Editing & Publications, I seek to provide the deep and thorough feedback I provide to clients as an editor at both Flash Fiction Magazine and Thirty West Publishing House.
