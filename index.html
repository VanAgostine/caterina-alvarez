Her story

Stories, people and writing

In 2016, I got an idea to write a book and started studying the art and craft of fiction like crazy. I got a few stories published and began reading the slush pile for several literary journals. Soon, authors I served as an editor began asking if I could help them with their works-in-progress.

I discovered I adore this type of work: I love stories and people and writing, and working on my own time, in spaces of my choice, often from far-flung places.
