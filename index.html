Hello

Ms. Alvarez’s work appears in The Los Angeles Review, The Atticus Review, Flash Boulevard and other journals. Her story “Short Division” won a spot on the Wigleaf Top 50 in 2023. She is a priority editor with Flash Fiction Magazine and senior editor at Thirty West Publishing House. A longtime member of The Lighthouse Writers Workshop, she received her masters at USC in 2000.

With Grand Avenue Editing and Publication, she seeks to reach a broader audience for her publications and to provide the deep and thorough feedback she provides to clients as an editor at both Flash Fiction Magazine and Thirty West Publishing House. Focusing on both the strengths and weaknesses of your work, Caterina finds ways to marry your intention for your work with a technical foundation that will free your stories to be fully realized.

Your options are, of course, limitless, but my gut tells me the heart lies right here. This is the axis upon which your story spins.

Developmental editing for writers and their stories, and a small, growing collection of publications of my own.
