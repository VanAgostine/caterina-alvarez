Her story

Stories, people and writing

In 2016, I got an idea to write a book and started studying the art and craft of fiction like crazy. I got a few stories published and began reading the slush pile for several literary journals. One of them, an indie press, hired me as an editor in 2022.

Soon, authors whom I served asked if I could help them on the side with their works-in-progress. I discovered I adore this type of work: I love stories and people and writing and working on my own time in spaces of my choice, often from far-flung places.
